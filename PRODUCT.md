# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Marketing leaders, founders, product teams, and development partners at ambitious English-speaking companies in Europe and the United States, with a Croatian-language path for local teams. They are preparing a launch, repositioning, migration, or next stage of organic growth and need to understand whether Koupoli is the right specialist to involve.

## Product Purpose

Koupoli explains and qualifies two offers: organic launch consultation and hands-on SEO and AI-search operations. The site helps prospective clients understand the work, review experience and published guidance, and send a considered enquiry.

## Positioning

Koupoli connects organic-growth strategy to technical delivery in one specialist-led engagement. Its work spans technical SEO, information architecture, content planning, structured data, migrations, Webflow delivery, and AI-search visibility.

## Operating Context

The public site is bilingual: English is the primary version and Croatian mirrors it under `/hr/`. Visitors use Projects, About, Notes, the Glossary, guide pages, FAQs, and an enquiry form to evaluate fit. The site is published statically from GitHub Pages on koupoli.com.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- React and Vite static site with pre-rendered routes, sitemap, hreflang, canonical metadata, and JSON-LD.
- All published media must be bundled as first-party assets.
- Enquiries deliver privately to karlo.rida@gmail.com; the email address is not displayed publicly.
- English and Croatian routes must remain equivalent where content exists.

## Brand Commitments

Use the Koupoli name, supplied bird mark/favicon, and established blue and paper-based brand palette. Voice is direct, specific, calm, and practitioner-led. Avoid generic marketing claims, decorative AI motifs, and em dashes.

## Evidence on Hand

The site contains real client projects, Karlo Ridan's documented work history, bilingual Notes, a glossary, first-party illustrations, and supplied brand assets. Do not invent results, client testimonials, customers, pricing, or capabilities.

## Product Principles

1. Explain organic growth as connected strategy and delivery, not isolated SEO tasks.
2. Make technical and AI-search concepts useful to a buyer, not merely visible as keywords.
3. Support a considered decision with real projects, plain language, and relevant next steps.
4. Keep every English and Croatian pathway consistent and easy to return from.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast requirements, use body copy at 16px or larger, maintain visible focus states, provide useful image alternatives, and support keyboard navigation across desktop and mobile layouts.
