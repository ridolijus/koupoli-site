# SEO retainer scope and monthly delivery record

Use this record to make recurring work visible. It is a blank planning template, not a Koupoli price list, contract or client result.

## Agree the engagement

- Goal, priority pages and market:
- Included work and available capacity:
- Implementation included or handled by the client's developers:
- Named specialist, client approver and implementation owner:
- Approval process, communication cadence and handling of urgent work:
- Exclusions, tools or costs billed separately:
- Treatment of unused capacity and changes to scope:
- Review date, notice period, termination and handover arrangements:

## Choose this month's work

For each approved item, record the affected pages, reason for priority, intended technical or content change, owner, dependencies and acceptance test. Do not commit to more work than the agreed capacity supports.

## Record delivery

- Approved item:
- Work completed and evidence:
- Date deployed:
- Live verification and result:
- Waiting on client approval, development or another dependency:
- Remaining risk or next action:

Keep proposed, approved, deployed and verified work separate. A recommendation is not a completed fix.

## Review outcomes

Use comparable reporting windows and the same page, query, country and device filters where appropriate. Record Search Console impressions and clicks alongside meaningful enquiries or other conversion measures when tracking is available.

State what the data supports and what it does not. New rankings, AI citations or revenue are not guaranteed by a recurring fee. Sparse data, seasonality and other changes may prevent a reliable conclusion.

## Decide what happens next

Continue, change priorities, reduce capacity, move to a bounded project or end the engagement. Preserve the change log, access ownership and unresolved backlog for the handover.
