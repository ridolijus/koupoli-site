# Koupoli GEO working example

Prepared: November 7, 2026. Owner: Karlo Ridan. This is a content change on Koupoli's own service pages, not a client case or a completed AI visibility study. Preparation date is not the deployment date.

## Buyer question and target

Main question: What does a technical SEO engagement include?

English canonical: https://koupoli.com/services/technical-seo-ai-search/

Croatian equivalent: https://koupoli.com/hr/usluge/seo-optimizacija/

Keep separate performance records for the two URLs. A country's location does not establish the visitor's language.

## Five answer checks

1. What work is included? State the relevant diagnosis and technical areas, with scope agreed for the project.
2. Who does the work? Say that the buyer works directly with Karlo Ridan; explain how the buyer's developers may participate.
3. What should the buyer provide? Ask for the website URL, platform, important pages, business goal and currently available Search Console information. Arrange access securely after scope is agreed; do not ask for passwords through the enquiry form.
4. What does the buyer receive? Describe prioritised implementation work, responsibilities, acceptance criteria and an agreed handoff or implementation support.
5. How are scope and progress agreed? Set deliverables and a review method without inventing a fee, deadline or search guarantee.

These are editorial buyer-question checks. They are not presented as observed GSC queries or prompts proven to earn citations. No AI model trial has been performed for this example.

## The page change

Previously, the service pages described technical areas and delivery stages in separate sections. The change adds a direct engagement answer near the start, identifies Karlo as the delivery specialist, and states the starting information, outputs and scope limits. Two supporting FAQ answers clarify preparation and agreement of scope. The existing service URLs, enquiry route and delivery stages are retained.

The [implementation Note](https://koupoli.com/post/generative-engine-optimization-process/) explains the process. The service page supplies the visible offer. This is one connected example, not three interchangeable answers to the same intent.

## Evidence and baseline

The service content is the source for the offer. Karlo's professional background provides author context; neither is evidence of a particular ranking or AI citation.

Before release, save the previous service content and dated page-level Web performance for each exact canonical URL. Retain the Search Console property, complete date window, country/device filters and query-filter rules. Save AI-report availability and page-level impressions separately where available. Keep account exports and enquiries private.

Do not substitute whole-site data for a service-page baseline. Do not add AI impressions to Web totals. An unavailable value is not automatically a measured zero. The dedicated AI report does not attribute impressions to this question set.

## Release and observation

Record the actual deployment date and commit after approval. Confirm that the answer is present in live HTML, the final URLs return HTTP 200, the declared canonicals and language alternates still match, and the enquiry path works. Record Google index status, its selected canonical and the first observed post-release crawl separately in URL Inspection. Record the effective Search generative AI inclusion setting; an HTTP check alone does not establish it.

Use equal complete pre-change and post-change windows with the same supported filters and page aggregation. Exclude release day and preliminary dates. Log other releases, demand changes and recrawling as alternative explanations. A late-November review is a planning checkpoint, not a promised result date.

Post-release Web results: not measured in this preparation record.

Post-release AI results: not measured in this preparation record.

Qualified enquiries: not measured in Search Console; assess privately using a consistent enquiry definition.

## Decision

Keep the factual service clarification if it makes the offer easier to evaluate. Extend observation if the data is too sparse. Do not call a change in impressions a proven causal effect, and do not claim an AI citation uplift without repeatable evidence.
