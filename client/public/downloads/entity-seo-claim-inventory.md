## Worked structure for a specialist service business

| Entity | Primary page | Supporting evidence | Relationship to explain |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The organisation | Homepage or About | Contact details, service pages | Who provides the work |
| The specialist | About | Work history, published guidance | Who is responsible for the advice |
| The service | Service page | Process, scope, relevant guidance | What is delivered and for whom |
| A project | Projects page | Approved project description | Where the work has been applied |
| A practical concept | Guide or Note | Sources, method, template | Why the service approach is useful |

Do not create pages, credentials, outcomes, or relationships only to fill this sheet. Keep the inventory limited to information a visitor can verify and that the business can maintain.
