# Glossary research sources

Research gathered 6 October 2026 for Koupoli's Phase 1 glossary.

- Google, [Creating helpful, reliable, people-first content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content): Google recommends original, useful, people-first content and cautions against mass-produced, search-engine-first material.
- Google, [Optimizing your website for generative AI features on Google Search](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide): SEO foundations remain relevant to generative AI search. Google advises unique, non-commodity content and warns against separate pages for every possible query variation or special AI-only markup.
- Ahrefs, [SEO Glossary](https://ahrefs.com/seo/glossary): a broad alphabetical hub with 190+ terms and individual term URLs.
- Semrush, [The Ultimate SEO Glossary](https://www.semrush.com/kb/925-glossary): a 123-term central glossary, with some terms leading to deeper resources.
- Moz, [SEO Glossary of Terms](https://moz.com/beginners-guide-to-seo/seo-glossary): a glossary organized by SEO learning-path chapters.
- Semji, [GEO & SEO Glossary](https://semji.com/glossary): a focused 12-term single-page AI-search glossary tied to relevant services.
- Level Agency, [AI SEO Glossary](https://www.level.agency/ai-seo-glossary/): a category hub with dozens of individual term pages, related terms, practical guidance, and service CTAs.
