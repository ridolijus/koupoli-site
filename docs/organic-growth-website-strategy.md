# Koupoli organic growth website strategy

**Recommendation:** Position Koupoli as the partner that helps ambitious teams make their brand discoverable, understandable, and credible across classic search and AI-assisted search. The site should sell one discipline—**organic growth**—through two clear ways to work together:

1. **Organic Launch Advisory** for founders and marketing leads who need the plan, priorities, and decision support.
2. **SEO & AI Search Execution** for teams that need an operator to implement the technical, content, and search work.

“Launch your brand organically” is a strong positioning line. It communicates the outcome and differentiates the brand. It is not, however, a search term with established demand, so it should be the promise in the hero rather than the sole SEO target.

## What the market is asking for

Search is becoming a broader discovery system, not a replacement for SEO. Google’s own guidance is unusually clear: SEO fundamentals remain the basis for visibility in AI Overviews and AI Mode; there is no special AI markup, `llms.txt` file, or separate optimisation requirement for Google’s AI features. Pages need to be indexed, crawlable, useful, technically sound, and eligible for a normal search snippet. [1] [2]

This creates an opportunity for Koupoli. Many providers now sell “AI SEO” as a new, standalone tactic. The more credible position is: **AI search visibility is an extension of good organic marketing, not a bag of hacks.** The practical work remains technical crawlability, page experience, information architecture, original content, clear claims, authoritative evidence, and a coherent brand footprint. Google explicitly warns against scaled content made to chase every AI-query variation and against inauthentic mentions. [2]

The buyer’s problem is also changing. A traffic-only promise is less persuasive when AI answers resolve simpler queries before a click occurs. A better commercial promise is to build organic discovery that creates qualified visits, branded demand, citations, and conversion opportunities. Google says AI-feature traffic is included in Search Console’s Web reporting and advises site owners to assess conversion value and engagement instead of clicks alone. [1] Semrush’s July 2025 model, based on more than 500 digital-marketing and SEO topics, projects AI-search traffic could overtake traditional search traffic for that topic set by early 2028. That is a directional industry projection—not a universal forecast—but it supports treating AI visibility as part of the operating model now. [3]

The category is noisy. “AI SEO” has meaningful English-language demand, but the highest-volume variations are frequently tool-shopping queries rather than requests for consulting. In Ahrefs’ US dataset, `ai seo` shows 8,500 average monthly searches, but its parent topic is “seo key”; `ai seo tools` and similar terms dominate many related queries. Koupoli should use AI-search language to explain its service, not build the entire site around the phrase or compete as software. In contrast, `technical seo consultant` shows 1,500 average monthly searches and a difficulty score of 3 in the same dataset; this is much closer to the operational offer and the existing experience base.

## Recommended market position

> **Koupoli helps teams launch and grow brands organically through search strategy, technical SEO, content systems, and AI-search visibility.**

The positioning should be **English-first and international**. It is already the language of the existing site, Koupoli’s project history includes international work, and English service keywords offer materially stronger demand than a Croatia-only market. Croatia should be a focused secondary route only if local work is a real commercial priority. Ahrefs’ Croatia data shows demand for `SEO optimizacija` (700/month), `SEO agencija` (150/month), `tehnički SEO` (150/month), and `SEO savjetovanje` (100/month), which can support a dedicated Croatian landing page later without weakening the main brand.

Do not lead with “full-service organic marketing.” It implies services such as social, lifecycle, PR, and paid distribution that are not central to the stated delivery model. Lead instead with **organic launch**, **SEO strategy**, and **search execution**. The breadth comes from the system around search, not an unbounded service list.

## The two offers

### 1. Organic Launch Advisory

**Who it is for:** Founders, marketing leads, and in-house teams who have a product, a market, or an upcoming launch but need a disciplined organic plan before resources are spent in the wrong places.

**What it solves:** “We know organic search matters, but we do not know what to build first, how to position it, which audience questions matter, or what our internal team should own.”

**Core outputs:**

- Market, audience, and search-demand map.
- Positioning and messaging recommendations for search-led discovery.
- Technical and information-architecture priorities.
- Keyword, topic, and entity map tied to the buyer journey.
- Content roadmap, page briefs, and distribution priorities.
- AI-search visibility baseline and measurement plan.
- A prioritised 90-day launch plan with owner-ready briefs.

**Commercial shape:** A fixed-scope launch sprint, followed by optional monthly advisory. This is easier to buy and easier to explain than open-ended consulting.

### 2. SEO & AI Search Execution

**Who it is for:** Teams that have the strategic direction but want a specialist to run the work with their developers, writers, designers, or agency partners.

**What it solves:** “We need an operator who can make the website technically sound, shape the content system, and move search visibility forward without creating a separate AI-growth theatre.”

**Core work:**

- Technical SEO, crawlability, indexation, Core Web Vitals, and migration support.
- Information architecture, internal linking, canonicalisation, and structured data.
- Keyword and entity research, content briefs, on-page optimisation, and content refreshes.
- Search-ready Webflow and CMS implementation.
- AI-search readiness: clear sourceable information, differentiated expert content, brand consistency, and monitoring of relevant prompts and citations.
- Reporting that connects visibility to qualified visits, leads, and commercial actions.

**Commercial shape:** A monthly operating engagement with a defined backlog, clear delivery cadence, and access to the right internal stakeholders.

## Recommended site map

The new website should make the engagement choice immediately obvious while keeping **Projects** and **About** at their existing URLs and with their current dark layout, typography, and colour system.

| Route | Job of the page | Primary action |
|---|---|---|
| `/` | Explain the positioning, establish credibility, and route visitors to the appropriate offer. | Choose advisory or execution. |
| `/organic-launch-advisory` | Sell the strategy-first offer, its outputs, process, and fit. | Start a launch conversation. |
| `/seo-ai-search-execution` | Sell the operating offer, its scope, working model, and technical depth. | Discuss an execution engagement. |
| `/how-we-work` | Explain the shared process, decision points, collaboration model, and measurement. | See the process or contact Koupoli. |
| `/projects` | Preserve current layout and colours; gradually add clearer context and outcomes when verified. | View relevant work. |
| `/about` | Preserve current layout and colours; use it to substantiate specialist credibility and working approach. | Start a conversation. |
| `/insights` | Build topical authority through a small, opinionated library rather than a high-volume blog. | Read a practical guide. |
| `/contact` | Convert qualified visitors with a brief, low-friction enquiry flow. | Send an enquiry. |

A compact navigation should be: **Services**, **How we work**, **Projects**, **About**, **Insights**, and a high-contrast **Start your organic launch** button. Services should expand to the two offers, rather than forcing visitors to infer the difference from a generic “Services” page.

### Homepage section order

1. **Hero** — “Launch your brand organically.” Clarify that Koupoli combines search strategy, technical SEO, content planning, and AI-search visibility. Present the two engagement paths side by side.
2. **The shift in discovery** — A concise, evidence-led explanation that brands now need to be discoverable in Google Search and AI-assisted search. Avoid fear messaging and unsupported visibility promises.
3. **Two ways to work together** — Two distinct, equal cards: Advisory and Execution. Each should show client fit, key outputs, and an individual CTA.
4. **What organic launch actually includes** — A systems map: positioning, technical foundation, content system, discoverability, and measurement. This communicates range without inventing extra services.
5. **Proof** — Selected projects, relevant experience, tools, and verifiable work types. Use real project logos already in the repository, but only make outcome claims that can be documented.
6. **How the work moves** — Discover, prioritise, build, measure, improve. This should lead to the working-method page.
7. **Opinionated point of view** — A short statement: “AI search is not a shortcut around SEO. It raises the standard for it.” Link to the AI-search explainer.
8. **Final conversion section** — State who the work is best for, show the email/contact route, and invite the visitor to choose advisory or execution.

### Pages to retain or retire

- Keep **Projects** and **About** structurally and visually intact. Their current shared system—near-black `#171715`, charcoal surfaces, white type, muted grey text, blue accent, Gabarito display type, and Sulphur Point body type—becomes the visual DNA for the new pages.
- Replace the current home page completely.
- Replace the existing one-post blog with **Insights**. The current AI-productivity article does not reinforce the new commercial positioning. It can be archived, rewritten with a stronger expert angle, or removed from the primary navigation. Do not build a content hub around broad AI commentary.
- Preserve legacy URLs where practical. If an old page is retired, redirect it to the closest relevant new destination. This protects existing signals and avoids a launch full of dead ends.

## Keyword strategy

Keyword targeting should be page-level and intent-led. The site should not attempt to rank for `seo services` or `seo agency` as a generalist. Those terms are broad, expensive, and less aligned to a specialist-led service. Ahrefs shows 57,000 US monthly searches and difficulty 50 for `seo services`, compared with more attainable specialist terms such as `technical seo consultant`.

### Primary commercial targets

| Page | Main target concept | Supporting language | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Homepage | SEO consultant; organic growth strategy | organic launch, search strategy, AI search visibility | Establishes the founder-led specialist position. |
| Organic Launch Advisory | SEO consulting; SEO strategy consultant | organic growth advisory, content strategy consultant, launch SEO strategy | Matches a planning-first buying decision. |
| SEO & AI Search Execution | technical SEO consultant | technical SEO services, website architecture, on-page SEO, Webflow SEO | Closest match to hands-on delivery and existing proof. |
| SEO & AI Search Execution | AI SEO services; AI SEO service | AI search optimisation, Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT Search | Captures the emerging category without posing as a software vendor. |
| Insight pillar | generative engine optimization | GEO, AEO, AI SEO strategy | A high-interest educational topic; use it to demonstrate judgment, not as the home-page headline. |
| Croatia route, only if strategic | SEO optimizacija; SEO savjetovanje | tehnički SEO, SEO agencija, SEO stručnjak | Local-language commercial demand; do not mix Croatian and English intent on the same core page. |

**Important filter:** Many `AI SEO` variations are tool-shopping queries. Avoid building product-style “best AI SEO tools” pages unless Koupoli will genuinely review or compare tools. Focus on queries that signal a service need: `ai seo services`, `ai seo service`, `technical seo consultant`, and `seo consulting`.

Use entities to make the site precise and easy to understand. Do not insert them as a checklist. They should appear naturally in service descriptions, proof, methodology, articles, case studies, structured data, and internal links.

| Entity group | Priority entities |

| Technical SEO | crawlability, indexation, XML sitemap, robots.txt, canonical URL, redirect mapping, Core Web Vitals, internal linking, structured data, Schema.org, information architecture |
| Content and strategy | search intent, topical authority, entity SEO, content brief, content refresh, editorial roadmap, conversion tracking, content distribution |
| Measurement | Google Analytics 4, Search Console, conversion rate, qualified leads, branded search, AI visibility, citation tracking |

Use vertical entities only where there is credible work to support them. Existing experience makes **Webflow**, **ecommerce**, **technical SEO**, **site migration**, and **international or multi-market work** plausible proof-led themes. Do not create a vertical landing page merely because the keyword exists.

## Content plan for authority and AI-search visibility

Start with four substantial, experience-led pieces. Each should answer a commercial question, contain a clear point of view, use examples from actual work where permission allows, and link to the relevant offer.

1. **What AI SEO actually means for a service business** — Separate useful AI-search work from `llms.txt`, mass-content, and other unsupported shortcuts.
2. **The organic launch framework for a new brand or website relaunch** — Positioning, demand, architecture, technical readiness, content, measurement, and first 90 days.
3. **Technical SEO before content: the launch checklist that prevents wasted work** — Crawlability, redirects, canonicalisation, internal links, schema, performance, analytics, and validation.
4. **How to decide between SEO advisory and an SEO operating partner** — A decision guide that routes readers to one of the two offers.

This is more useful than publishing frequent generic “SEO tips.” Google’s guidance emphasizes original, expert, non-commodity content, clear technical structure, and material that genuinely satisfies the reader. [2]

## Technical requirements for the redesign launch

The design can change completely without changing the brandbook. Preserve the current colour tokens and font families, but apply them to a more decisive conversion journey. The palette should remain near-black, charcoal, white, muted grey, and the existing blue accent—not a new colour system.

Before launch, the build should also satisfy the following:

- **Pre-render or statically generate core marketing routes.** The current app is a client-rendered React/Vite site. Google can process JavaScript when it is not blocked, but Google itself notes that JavaScript SEO is more complex. For a search-led site that should also be accessible to other retrieval systems, core content should ship as crawlable HTML at build time. [2]
- **Retain a stable URL map.** Keep `/about` and `/projects`; redirect retired pages to the closest equivalent; update the sitemap and internal links in the same release.
- **Add organisation and person clarity.** Use accurate Organization/ProfessionalService, Person, Service, BreadcrumbList, and Article structured data where the visible page content supports it. Do not add FAQ schema or special “AI schema” merely for AI visibility. Google says there is no special schema required for AI features. [1]
- **Make the contact path real.** The new site should either use a working form provider or make `hello@koupoli.com` the explicit low-friction primary action. Do not retain a decorative form that cannot deliver enquiries.
- **Instrument the decision funnel.** Track offer-card clicks, contact starts, email clicks, qualified enquiries, booked calls, and resulting revenue. Pair Search Console and GA4 with a monthly AI-search prompt and citation baseline. Search Console provides AI-feature performance reporting, but it should be interpreted alongside conversions and engagement. [1] [2]
- **Keep first-party assets.** The current project already serves migrated imagery from `koupoli.com`; retain that first-party approach for the redesign so media does not depend on Webflow URLs.
- **Validate performance and accessibility.** Mobile behaviour, focus states, contrast, descriptive alt text, image dimensions, and a light JavaScript footprint should be launch gates, not post-launch clean-up.

## Recommended next decision

Approve this direction before moving into design and code:

- **Brand promise:** “Launch your brand organically.”
- **Primary market:** English-first international service business, with Croatian SEO as a separate future route only if it supports the commercial plan.
- **Offers:** Organic Launch Advisory and SEO & AI Search Execution.
- **Preserved pages:** `/projects` and `/about`, visually retained with the current colour and layout system.
- **Primary conversion:** An enquiry or booking conversation, not a generic newsletter or free-tool lead magnet.

Once approved, the next phase should be a page-by-page content outline and visual direction, followed by implementation in the local codebase. Nothing should be pushed to GitHub until the implementation is reviewed and explicitly approved.
