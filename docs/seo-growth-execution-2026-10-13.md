## Outreach sequence

Start outreach only once the migration checklist or another evidence-led asset is public and internally linked.

1. **Create a short target list.** Prioritise real client acknowledgements, existing partner directories with an authentic Koupoli profile, Croatian and European marketing publications, conference or podcast opportunities, and editors whose readers would use the asset.
2. **Match the asset to the audience.** A migration checklist suits Webflow, development, technical-marketing, and redesign audiences. The outreach angle should explain the resource's specific use, not ask for a generic agency backlink.
3. **Make a specific first contact.** Reference a relevant article, event, or resource from the recipient. Offer the checklist as a useful source, guest contribution, or expert comment only when it genuinely fits their audience.
4. **Keep follow-up limited.** Use one concise follow-up after five to seven working days. Stop after a second unanswered contact.
5. **Record real outcomes.** Track target, relevance, contact date, asset offered, response, publication URL, link attribute where visible, and next action. Do not buy links or count low-quality spam domains as progress.

The six-month goal is five to ten legitimate, relevant referring domains. A manual-action check remains a monthly monitoring task; do not submit a disavow file unless there is a manual action or an unusually clear, material risk.

## Measurement cadence

| Timing | Check | Decision trigger |
|---|---|---|
| After the service-page release | Sitemap submission and live URL inspection | Confirm the pages are publicly crawlable before requesting indexing. |
| Seven days after release | Search Console URL Inspection for service pages | Check whether Google has discovered or indexed the new URLs. |
| Fourteen days after sitemap resubmission | Sitemap Last read and discovered URL count | Investigate only if the read date has not advanced or the count remains materially below the live sitemap. |
| Weekly for 30 days | Search Console impressions, clicks, CTR, and average position by English services, Croatian services, glossary/Notes, and proof pages | Improve titles and descriptions when a page receives enough impressions but underperforms on CTR. |
| Monthly | Manual Actions, Security Issues, genuine referring domains, and outreach log | Continue earning editorial references; do not react to background spam without a manual-action signal. |

## Guardrails

- SEO fundamentals, crawlability, original information, and clear site structure remain the basis for AI-search visibility.
- Do not create thin country or city landing pages for Balkan markets.
- Do not use AI-only schema, artificial mentions, or mass-produced long-tail pages as a substitute for substantive content.
- Preserve reciprocal English/Croatian hreflang links and canonical URLs whenever a new page pair is published.
