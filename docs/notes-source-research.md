# Official sources for Koupoli Notes expansion

## AI search visibility and measurement

Google states that generative AI features use core Search ranking and quality systems. Content should be original, useful, well-structured, crawlable, and supported with relevant images or video when appropriate. Google says there is no special AI markup, LLMS.txt file, or prescribed content chunking required for inclusion in its generative features. Search Console's Generative AI performance report provides impressions, pages, countries, devices for Search, and time-based reporting for visibility in AI features.

- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide
- https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/06/gen-ai-performance-reports

## Organic launch and technical foundations

Google Search Console guidance recommends site ownership verification, monitoring indexing coverage, submitting a sitemap where useful, monitoring performance, using URL Inspection for individual pages, checking structured data reports, and monitoring Core Web Vitals. Google also recommends clear, descriptive URLs, canonicalization to reduce duplicate content, readable organized text, and pages accessible to crawlers in the same form users see.

- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/monitor-debug/search-console-start
- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide
